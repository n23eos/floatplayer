FloatPlayer is ready

Three things to know, and you're done.

  1. 1

    Look for the button in the player

    On any YouTube video, a new button appears in the player controls, left of the settings gear. One click and the video pops out into a window that stays on top of everything.

  2. 2

    Or use the keyboard

    The shortcuts work from any Chrome window, so you can pop the video out without switching tabs.

  3. 3

    Everything else lives in the mini-window

    The panel at the top of the window holds the extras. Nothing to set up — they are all one click away.

    • Playback speed and A-B loop
    • Volume boost up to 300%
    • Night mode and a sleep timer
    • Sponsor segments on the timeline
    • Comments, live chat, recommendations and a queue
